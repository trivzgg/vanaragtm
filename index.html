How it works

From audit to
active pipeline.

A structured four-phase engagement that builds your GTM engine end to end. No guesswork, no wasted budget.

01
Sales Audit & Strategy

We start with a full audit of your current motion. What's working, what's leaking, and where the biggest opportunities live. ICP definition, TAM analysis, and a clear GTM strategy document come out of this phase.

Output: GTM Strategy Doc · ICP Definition · TAM Map
02
Infrastructure Build

Email domains acquired and warmed, mailboxes configured, DMARC/SPF/DKIM validated. Simultaneously, we build your Clay tables with contact enrichment, signal scoring, and buying-window detection logic baked in.

Output: Live Email Infrastructure · Clay Signal Tables · Enriched Contact Lists
03
Sequence Activation

Multi-channel sequences go live in Apollo. A/B tested subject lines, custom dynamic variables, and trigger-based logic tied to your signals. First pipeline appears within weeks, not quarters.

Output: Active Sequences · A/B Tests Running · First Meetings Booked
04
Optimise & Scale

Monthly reporting, diagnostic reviews, subject line iteration, and signal refinement. Marlene manages warm leads as they surface. The engine compounds. The longer it runs, the better it performs.

Output: Monthly Report · Optimised Sequences · Growing Pipeline
